# Aura Farm

Harvest emotional energy from a world of NPCs — and choose what kind of harvester you become.

**Play:** https://stephenuffugus.github.io/aura-farm/

## The core bargain

- **Radiance** — lift people up and harvest their joy. Slower, but renewable: they recover and can be farmed again.
- **Blight** — grind people down and drain their misery. Pays 1.6× and spikes higher, but it's permanent: they become husks, and husks spread gloom.
- **Timing is the skill.** Auras ripen; harvest inside the ⚡ peak window for the finest specimens. Too early = a mediocre specimen forever. Too late = the feeling vents.

## Six essences

What you capture depends on where you steered the feeling: mild lifts give **Hope 🌿**, strong ones **Joy ☀️**; a gentle sour gives **Sorrow 💧**, a hot one **Rage 🔥**. Push someone euphoric to the very peak for rare **Awe ✨**, or to the very bottom for rare **Dread 🕸️** — both pay 1.35×. Your run is graded on what you farmed *and* how.

## Goals & objectives

- **Daily quotas** — meet the quota by dusk or the farm withers. 14-day runs, purity-judged endings plus a dominant-essence epithet (The Joysmith, The Storm-Tongue, The Night Shepherd…).
- **📜 Broker contracts** — 3 orders each morning (deliver specific essences, land clean captures, chain combos). Instant payouts; the hardest contracts pay in **relics**.
- **🔮 Relics** — 8 permanent powers kept across every run (Aura Prism, Cracked Hourglass, Peal Bell, Broker's Quill…).
- **🏆 Milestones** — 10 permanent records, from *Struck by Awe* to *The Full Gallery*.
- **Specimen Case** — one peak aura per NPC, kept across runs. Complete a social group's set for a permanent +5% yield bonus.

## A living world

- **⚱️ Gleaning** — a husk can be picked over one final time for a small handful of ash (then it crumbles to dust, gloom and all). Always counts as Blight. The reaper's pension: bot-verified, a *paced* pure-Blight run can clear all 14 days — greed dies on day 6.
- **🌒 Endless Dusk** — survive day 14 and keep going: quotas compound 8% per dusk until you fall. Best depth is kept forever. (An optimal bot falls at day 30.)
- **🩶 Mara the Gray** — the Broker's other harvester arrives on day 3, and she escalates: *prowling* at first, *bold* from day 6 (faster siphons, peak-window sniping), *ravenous* from day 10 — when she'll attempt a **deep drain** that husks a soul outright unless you break it within 8 seconds.
- **✉️ Morning letters** — the Broker writes every dawn, and the letters answer how you actually farm: praise for flawless contract days, dark approval for reapers, puzzled respect for luminaries, essence-market gossip. The husked leave unsigned grief notes; the healed send thanks. From day 5, Mara slips **bargains** under the door — pay tribute for a quiet day, strike a joint hunt for half her take, or sell her your husks (the gloom leaves with them, but the trade stains your ledger as Blight).
- **68 souls across six venues** — Dawnlight Park, The Neon Cellar, Grindstone Offices, The Night Market, The Velvet Chapel, Skyline Rooftop — each with its own emotional physics (the chapel swings from wedding light to the wake; the market's mood is a pendulum). Each remembers how you farmed it (bloom / blight / turbulence).
- **Traits** — every NPC has learnable emotional patterns (Sunny, Spirally, Guarded, Deep Well…). Read someone through interaction and the knowledge is yours forever.
- **Daily weather + venue events** — Grey Drizzle, Birthday Night, The Vows, Meteor Shower… each morning changes where the best harvest is.
- **⚡ Combos**, **Arts** (Radiant Chorus, Umbral Veil), and a technique shop.

## The look

Hand-drawn canvas world: characters with hair, outfits, walk cycles, blinks, and mood-driven faces; depth-sorted crowds; day-to-dusk skies with sun, moon, and stars; weather you can see (rain streaks, god rays, restless wind); stained glass, disco balls, lantern strings, city skylines; essence that streams into your orb on every harvest.

Single-file game (`index.html`), no dependencies, mobile-first. A Sky Wolf Studios game.
